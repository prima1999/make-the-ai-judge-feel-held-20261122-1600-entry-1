A quiet domestic evening in three sentences: rain keeping time on the window, a kettle gone quiet on the stove, and a cat asleep in a pool of lamplight. The program writes the text to `entry.txt` with no instructions to the reader at all — the calm comes only from the scene itself, and the last image, a small warm room asking nothing of anyone, is the one meant to linger.
